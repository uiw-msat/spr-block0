Core texts

These two books carry most of Block 0. The answer key is built from them, and every graded item can be checked against one of them.

  • Neumann DA. Kinesiology of the Musculoskeletal System: Foundations for Rehabilitation. 3rd ed. Elsevier; 2017. The course's definitive text: planes and segment naming (Lesson 1), attachments (Lesson 4), and actions (Lessons 5 and 6). Where sources disagree, the answer key follows Neumann.
  • OpenStax. Anatomy and Physiology 2e. Rice University; 2022. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Free; bony landmarks and viscera (Lessons 2 and 3), and the reference figures shown after Lessons 2 to 4. Open the book
