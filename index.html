Block 0 · Anatomy and gait foundations

Build the reference you will use in every lab

Ten short lessons produce two documents the in-person units run on: a triplanar action table for the course's muscles and a Rancho Los Amigos gait-phase chart. When you finish, the site assembles your answers into a printable Field Atlas and issues a completion certificate for Canvas.

Every item is checked against the course answer key. A wrong answer is marked, not revealed, and each lesson links to the exact textbook section where the answer is. If you know this material, you will move quickly; if you need to look things up, the references are there.

Before you start

  1. Use one computer and one browser. Your answers are saved only in the browser you use; nothing is stored on a server. To switch computers or browsers, click Export progress below, then Import progress on the new one. Clearing your browser data erases your work, and a private window loses it when you close the window.
  2. Use a laptop or desktop. Most lessons work on a tablet, but the Lesson 8 gait chart is not built for phones.

Your progress and your data

This site has no server, accounts, or analytics. Your answers live only in this browser and in any progress file you export. The instructors see your work only through the certificate and progress file you upload to Canvas.

What this is, and is not

The course introduces clinical vocabulary from the Postural Restoration Institute and credits it on the About page. It is not a PRI course and does not lead to PRI certification. Where the course states a clinical model rather than an established finding, it says so.
