About this site and its sources

Attribution

The terms Left AIC, PEC, AF and FA, zone of apposition, and the pelvic orientation convention used in this course come from the Postural Restoration Institute (PRI). They are used here with credit, to prepare students for PRI's Myokinematic Restoration and Postural Respiration courses. This site does not reproduce PRI course materials, is not affiliated with or endorsed by PRI, and completing it confers no PRI credential.

Anatomy figures are from OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e (Rice University, CC BY 4.0) or Wikimedia Commons under the license shown with each figure. Gait and atlas diagrams are original.

Evidence and model

Three premises carry the course, and each rests on literature outside PRI: resting asymmetry is common and patterned; the diaphragm is a postural muscle as well as a respiratory one; and walking is reciprocal alternating activity organized by spinal circuits. The full PRI account of how those facts produce a specific resting pattern is a clinical model with case-level support. The course teaches it as a model and marks the difference where it matters.

Accessibility

Every input can be reached and answered with the keyboard. Right and wrong are shown with text as well as color. The site is designed for screens 1280 pixels wide and larger; most lessons work on a tablet, and the gait chart lesson is not intended for phones. Contact the instructors if anything blocks you.

Your data

The site has no server, accounts, or analytics. Answers are stored only in your browser and in progress files you choose to export and upload to Canvas.
